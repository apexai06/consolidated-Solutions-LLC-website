Who We Are

A Holding Company Built for the New Global Economy

Consolidated Solutions LLC is a U.S.-registered umbrella company headquartered in Casa Grande, Arizona, with a Nigerian subsidiary, Consolidated Solutions LTD, serving as the regional hub for African operations. Together, the dual-arm structure creates a seamless transatlantic corridor for capital, talent, and technology between the world's largest economy and Africa's most dynamic market.

Our structure is not a workaround — it is a deliberate architecture. It allows us to operate with U.S. institutional credibility while maintaining deep operational roots in West Africa, giving partners access to both markets through a single, trusted relationship. The portfolio spans energy, security, financial services, logistics, and global trade, with a strategic focus on scaling high-impact businesses in emerging markets.

We are builders, operators, and integrators. We do not merely advise — we deploy capital, build infrastructure, and run the systems that power the next generation of cross-border enterprise. Our ventures include Apex AI Solutions, an intelligent automation subsidiary, and Icend, a door-to-door shipping platform purpose-built for the Nigerian diaspora in the United States.

Global Reach

Two Continents. One Operating System.

USA

United States — Consolidated Solutions LLC

Registered in the United States, our LLC arm provides institutional credibility, access to U.S. capital markets, government contracting eligibility, and a base for technology and AI operations serving both domestic and international clients.

NGA

Nigeria — Consolidated Solutions LTD

Our Nigerian subsidiary operates at the intersection of Africa's largest economy and its most dynamic entrepreneurial ecosystem. From Lagos to Abuja, we deploy capital, talent, and technology where the opportunity is greatest and the need is most urgent.

The Corridor Advantage

The U.S.–Africa corridor represents one of the most underserved and highest-potential trade routes in the global economy. Consolidated Solutions is purpose-built to operate this corridor with the speed, compliance, and cultural intelligence that neither purely U.S. nor purely African firms can match alone.
